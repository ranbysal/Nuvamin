The company

We built Nuvamin to be
worthy of your trust.

Nuvamin was founded on a simple observation: in most laboratories, a surprising share of the work is re-checking the materials that arrive. A reagent that is two percent less pure than its label, or a standard that has quietly absorbed water, can cost weeks of experiments.

So we built a molecular library, not a catalogue. Every material carries a permanent NVM code, ships in the same Nuvamin 2R vial, and is characterised lot by lot — identity by NMR and LC-MS, purity by HPLC, water by Karl Fischer — before it is released.

We are deliberately selective. Rather than listing thousands of products we cannot stand behind, we keep a short library of cofactors, substrates, standards and reagents we can characterise thoroughly, and we publish the evidence for every lot.

The result is a supplier built around reproducibility: the same vial, the same label, the same documentation, and the same standard of analysis, whether you order one vial or one hundred.

About certificates
(01)

Identity

Structure confirmed by 1H-NMR and high-resolution LC-MS, then compared against retained reference spectra for the material.

(02)

Purity

Chromatographic purity by HPLC-UV, or charged-aerosol detection for materials without a chromophore. Method and result are printed on the certificate.

(03)

Content

Every vial is filled gravimetrically on a calibrated microbalance and sealed under dry nitrogen or argon, with water content measured by Karl Fischer titration.

(04)

Traceability

Lot → material → analysis → release. Certificates, raw data and retained samples are archived for five years under each lot number.
