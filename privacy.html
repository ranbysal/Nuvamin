Privacy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

Nuvamin ("we", "us") operates this website and supplies research materials to laboratories and institutions. You can reach us at support@nuvamin.bio.

2. What we collect

  • Order details. Your name, email address, delivery address, the items you order and your order totals.
  • Correspondence. Messages you send us by email or through the contact form, and your email address if you subscribe to the lot report.
  • Cart and preferences. Your cart and preferred shop view, kept in your browser’s local storage.
  • Technical information. Basic server logs such as IP address, browser type and access times, kept by our hosting provider.

3. Why we process it

We use personal data to process and deliver orders, send confirmations and certificates of analysis, answer support requests, keep business and tax records, and keep the website secure. Depending on the activity, we rely on performance of a contract, legal obligations, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the business, or consent where required. Nuvamin operates under the applicable laws of Ohio and the United States of America; additional privacy laws may apply based on where you live.

4. Who receives it

Our payment processor (to take payment), our shipping carriers (to deliver), our email provider (to send confirmations and receipts) and our hosting provider (to run the site). Each receives only the information it needs to do its job and is required to protect it. We may also disclose information where required by law, court order or a valid government request.

5. How long we keep it

Order records are kept for five years from the date of purchase for accounting, tax and product traceability. Other data is deleted or anonymized once we no longer need it for the reason it was collected.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email support@nuvamin.bio. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You may also lodge a complaint with the Ohio Attorney General or the privacy or data-protection authority responsible for your place of residence.

7. Cookies & local storage

The site uses your browser’s local and session storage to remember your cart, your preferred shop view and your most recent order. We do not currently set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If our use of cookies or analytics changes, this policy will be updated before those tools are used where prior notice or consent is required.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International processing

Your information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. Where applicable law requires it, we use appropriate contractual or organisational safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, providers or legal obligations. The current version will remain available on this page, and the date above identifies the month of the latest published version.
