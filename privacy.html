Privacy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

Nuvamin ("we", "us") operates this website, a concept store for precision molecular materials. You can reach us at support@nuvamin.bio.

In short: this website does not send your details anywhere. Cart contents and checkout details stay in your own browser and are used only to show your mock order confirmation.

2. What we collect

  • Checkout details — the name, email and delivery address you enter at checkout are kept in your browser’s session storage to display your mock order confirmation, and are cleared when you close the tab. They are not transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • Cart and preferences — your cart and your preferred library view are kept in your browser’s local storage.
  • Correspondence — messages you send us by email. The contact and newsletter forms on this site are demonstrations and do not transmit data.
  • Technical information — our hosting provider may keep basic server logs such as IP address, browser type and access times.

3. Why we process it

We use personal data to respond to support requests, maintain business records, secure the website and prevent abuse. Depending on the activity, we rely on performance of a contract, legal obligations, our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the business, or consent where required. Nuvamin operates under the applicable laws of Ohio and the United States of America; additional privacy laws may apply based on where you live.

4. Who receives it

Our hosting provider (to run the site) and our email provider (to answer messages you send us). Each receives only the information reasonably necessary to perform its service and is expected to protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information where required by law, court order or a valid government request.

5. How long we keep it

Data held in your browser stays under your control: session storage is cleared when you close the tab, and you can clear local storage at any time from your browser settings. Correspondence is deleted when it is no longer needed for the purpose for which it was sent.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise any of these rights, email support@nuvamin.bio. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. You may also lodge a complaint with the Ohio Attorney General or the privacy or data-protection authority responsible for your place of residence.

7. Cookies & local storage

The site uses browser local and session storage to remember your cart, your preferred library view and your most recent mock order. We do not currently set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If our use of cookies or analytics changes, this policy will be updated before those tools are used where prior notice or consent is required.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International processing

Your information may be processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate. Where applicable law requires it, we use appropriate contractual or organisational safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, providers or legal obligations. The current version will remain available on this page, and the date above identifies the month of the latest published version.
