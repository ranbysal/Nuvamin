02 / Process

Inside the lab

(01) Intake

Every lot starts in quarantine

Raw material arrives with the supplier’s paperwork. We seal it off and hold it until our own tests are done.

(02) Identity

We confirm exactly what it is

1H-NMR and high-resolution LC-MS check the structure against the reference spectra we keep for every material.

(03) Filling

Weighed into one standard vial

Each vial is filled by weight on a calibrated microbalance, then sealed under argon and crimped.

(04) Release

Released with its evidence

QC reviews the HPLC data and signs the certificate. The lot number on the label links back to it for five years.

03 / Why Nuvamin

Research deserves
certainty.
We test every lot before it ships.

(01)

Identity

We confirm the structure of every lot by 1H-NMR and high-resolution LC-MS, and check it against the reference spectra we keep for each material.

(02)

Purity

HPLC purity is measured on every lot. The method, detection wavelength and result are printed on the certificate.

(03)

Traceability

The lot number on each vial links back to the full analytical record for that batch, from intake to release.

(04)

Stability

Vials are filled under dry nitrogen or argon and kept at the conditions on the label right up until they ship.
