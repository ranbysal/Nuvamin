02 — Why Nuvamin

Research deserves
certainty.
Every lot is characterised before release.

(01)

Identity

Structure is confirmed on every lot by 1H-NMR and high-resolution LC-MS, then compared against our retained reference spectra.

(02)

Purity

Chromatographic purity by HPLC, with the method, wavelength and chromatogram published on the lot’s certificate.

(03)

Traceability

Lot → material → analysis → release. Every vial carries a lot number that resolves to its full analytical record.

(04)

Stability

Filled under dry nitrogen or argon, sealed in the same 2R vial, and stored to the conditions printed on each label.

04 — The Nuvamin 2R system

One vial.
Every material.

Identical glass, cap and label — only the molecule changes
(01)

One standard vial

Every material ships in the same 2R borosilicate vial with a crimped aluminium seal, so racks, freezers and inventory systems handle the whole library the same way.

(02)

Colour-coded families

A single band across the label identifies the family at a glance. The NVM code, lot and grade sit in the same place on every vial.

(03)

Standard formats

10, 25 and 50 mg presentations across the catalogue, each weighed gravimetrically and sealed under inert gas for consistent performance from lot to lot.

Every lot is characterised identically, regardless of order size. For research use only — not for use in diagnostic or therapeutic procedures.
